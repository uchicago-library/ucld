Priorities

  • Our most important users are from our academia, but the website needs to be usable by non-experts, new students, potential donors, and community members (neighborhood residents, high school teachers and students, etc).
  • We would like to provide customization where possible.
  • Ensure that Library sites are accessible and follow practices of universal design.

Reviewing Design Decisions

  • Brand palette — every brand color family, with its values, generated from the stylesheet.
  • Color Usage Guidelines — how much of each color to use, and for what.
  • Design Standards — the usability and accessibility requirements every component is held to.
  • Design Intent — where the system currently leans between competing values.

Building with the system rather than reviewing it? Start from Implementation.
